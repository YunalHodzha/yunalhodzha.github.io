# Rune Spire Ops privacy policy

Effective date: 17 October 2026

Rune Spire Ops is a private tool that the developer of the game Rune Spire, Yunal Hodzha, runs on their own computer. Nobody else can sign in to it or use it.

## What it accesses

When the developer signs in with Google, Rune Spire Ops asks for read-only access to:

- the mailbox of runespiregame@gmail.com, the game's contact address (Gmail, read-only);
- the game's AdMob reports (AdMob, read-only).

It cannot send email, change AdMob settings or reach any other account.

## How it uses the data

It shows the developer alerts and short summaries, such as the sender and subject of a new message or a drop in ad revenue. The text of messages is not sent to any AI service. Report numbers may be summarized with Anthropic's Claude.

## Storage and sharing

The data is stored only on the developer's computer. It is not sold, not shared and not used for advertising. Rune Spire Ops's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

## Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions). Questions: runespiregame@gmail.com.
